Chapter 1

Reframing billing in Google Ads

Billing was an afterthought — a place advertisers visited after the money was already spent. This vision turns it into the front door: an agentic surface that translates Google's taxonomy into each advertiser's, tracks ROI as it happens, and acts on their behalf. The framework is being adopted across Ads.

Chapter 2

Responsible feature access in Ads

Every new advertiser arrived with the same access — full keys to the platform from day one — even though abuse clustered around a small set of risky features. This work introduced progressive trust: feature access that earns itself over time, shaped jointly with safety partners. The framework now anchors how Ads thinks about risk at sign-up.

Chapter 3

Cases and Communities in Ads

Help lived outside the product — tickets in email, communities on a separate platform — so users left Ads to get unstuck, often opening duplicates because they couldn't find what they'd already filed. This work brought both inside, closing a loop that broke every time a user left. First of its kind at Google; now scaling across Advertiser Platforms.

Chapter 4

Agentic invoice reconciliation

Reconciling invoices was manual, fragile work — discrepancies traced one at a time, support tickets piling up while finance teams worked across disconnected tools. This vision proposes an AI agent that cross-checks invoice data, surfaces mismatches, and guides resolution where the work happens. Foundation for AI-led finance across Ads Billing and Payments.
